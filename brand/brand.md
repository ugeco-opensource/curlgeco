# curlgeco Brand Guide

## Brand promise
curlgeco is a sharp AI playground: local-first, operator-friendly, and deployment-aware. The visual system should feel technical without looking sterile.

## Primary palette
- Carbon black: `#111111`
- Deep workspace black: `#080808`
- Signal yellow: `#FFD54F`
- Amber accent: `#F59E0B`
- Warm ivory: `#FFF9E8`
- Text light: `#F8F3E6`
- Muted light: `rgba(248,243,230,0.68)`

## Logo rules
- Primary wordmark: `curl` in black, `geco` in yellow.
- The supplied SVG uses an ivory chip so the black `curl` word remains legible on dark or transparent surfaces.
- In the live UI, the app uses an inverse-friendly component wordmark while keeping the same color intent.

## Typography
- Product font: Space Grotesk
- Mono font: IBM Plex Mono
- Tone: assertive, clean, technical

## Product UI direction
- Dark-by-default workspace with warm yellow highlights instead of generic blue SaaS gradients.
- Borders should be soft and warm, not cold gray.
- Use layered glass panels sparingly, with strong hierarchy and generous spacing.

## Required copy hooks
- Footer must read: `curlgeco by ugeco`
- Public host: `curlgeco.ugeco.in`
- Product descriptor: `AI playground`

## Assets
- Primary logo: `brand/curlgeco.logo.svg`
- App-served logo copy: `curlgeco_frontend/public/curlgeco.logo.svg`
